    Phantom Beat-Breakers (2026)

    A rhythm-action game where you play as Lyra, an aspiring musician exploring the haunted manor of the long-dead legendary composer Montague Beathaven Dance in time with the beat while fending off spectral adversaries and collecting musical artifacts.

    virtual reality experience

    Nested (2025)

    An immersive virtual reality experience created for the Meta Quest headset. Inspired by the Thousand And One Nights. The work interrogates what it means for something to be “virtual” or “real”, and to offer the perspective that our reality is relative and, as the title suggests, nested.

    This project was exhibited at Interactions (2025) at Cornell University's Center for Teaching Innovation in Ithaca, New York.
